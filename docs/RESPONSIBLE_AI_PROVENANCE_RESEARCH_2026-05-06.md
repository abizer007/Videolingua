# Responsible AI & Provenance Research

Access date: 2026-05-06

This research supports Vidiolingua's responsible AI and provenance readiness layer. It is not legal advice and does not establish legal certification, full statutory compliance, C2PA certification, tamper-proof watermarking, or complete abuse prevention.

## Sources

### MeitY / PIB - SGI, deepfakes, and IT Rules developments

- Source title: "MeitY issues advisory to all intermediaries to comply with existing IT rules"
- URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1990542
- What it supports: India has treated deepfakes and AI-powered misinformation as safety and trust concerns under intermediary due-diligence framing.
- Used in frontend copy: Yes.
- Confidence: High for policy positioning; PIB is an official Government of India source.
- Limitations: Advisory/intermediary framing is not the same as a direct certification rule for Vidiolingua outputs.

### PIB - Government of India deepfake and misinformation position

- Source title: "Government of India committed towards preventing dissemination of misinformation including deep fakes on Internet"
- URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2039640
- What it supports: Official acknowledgment that deepfakes are AI-powered misinformation and that impersonation/misinformation risks are within India's online safety discourse.
- Used in frontend copy: Yes.
- Confidence: High for policy context.
- Limitations: Does not define a complete product compliance checklist.

### PIB - 2026 SGI / synthetically generated information developments

- Source title: "Press Release Page | Press Information Bureau" on SGI/deepfake amendments and advisories
- URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2245053
- What it supports: India-aware synthetic media governance readiness, clear labelling, traceable metadata, impersonation, election/user harm, and child-safety concerns around synthetically generated information.
- Used in frontend copy: Yes.
- Confidence: High, with date-sensitive caveat.
- Limitations: This project implements readiness artifacts only; it does not claim compliance with every operative obligation.

### MeitY - Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

- Source title: "Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023"
- URL: https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf
- What it supports: Personal data, processing, consent/withdrawal, breach, and erasure concepts relevant to consent records and retention metadata.
- Used in frontend copy: Yes.
- Confidence: High; official MeitY PDF.
- Limitations: The compliance engine does not determine whether every job involves personal data or biometric data; it minimizes collection and records retention intent.

### PIB / PRS / MeitY references - DPDP Rules, 2025

- Source title: "DPDP Rules, 2025 Notified"
- URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?id=156054&NoteId=156054&ModuleId=3&reg=3&lang=2
- Supporting source: https://prsindia.org/policy/monthly-policy-review/november-2025
- What it supports: Rules were notified with phased implementation and operational detail for notices, consent withdrawal, breach reporting, erasure, and board procedures.
- Used in frontend copy: Yes.
- Confidence: Medium-high; PIB/PRS references verify notification and implementation themes, while exact obligations require legal review.
- Limitations: The frontend avoids saying Vidiolingua is DPDP compliant.

### CERT-In - Directions under Section 70B

- Source title: "Directions by CERT-In under Section 70B, Information Technology Act 2000"
- URL: https://www.cert-in.org.in/Directions70B.jsp
- Supporting FAQ: https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/FAQs_on_CyberSecurityDirections_May2022.pdf
- What it supports: Cyber incident reporting and log/evidence preservation matter for audit trails and incident readiness. The FAQ discusses 6-hour reporting for qualifying incidents.
- Used in frontend copy: Yes.
- Confidence: High for official CERT-In direction existence.
- Limitations: Compliance passport is not an incident reporting system.

### C2PA - Content provenance standard

- Source title: "Content Credentials: C2PA Technical Specification 2.2"
- URL: https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.2/specs/C2PA_Specification.html
- What it supports: Provenance manifests can describe asset origin and edits. C2PA is the relevant content provenance standard.
- Used in frontend copy: Yes.
- Confidence: High for technical standard context.
- Limitations: Vidiolingua currently writes a C2PA-style sidecar only. It does not sign C2PA manifests or claim C2PA certification.

### NIST - Synthetic content transparency

- Source title: "Reducing Risks Posed by Synthetic Content: An Overview of Technical Approaches to Digital Content Transparency"
- URL: https://www.nist.gov/publications/reducing-risks-posed-synthetic-content-overview-technical-approaches-digital-content
- What it supports: Provenance, labeling, watermarking, detection, testing, auditing, and synthetic content transparency are recognized technical approaches, with limitations.
- Used in frontend copy: Yes.
- Confidence: High; official NIST publication.
- Limitations: NIST is U.S. guidance and not Indian law.

### NIST - Generative AI Risk Management Profile

- Source title: "Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile"
- URL: https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence
- What it supports: Risk management and lifecycle controls for generative AI should be voluntary, contextual, and governance-oriented.
- Used in frontend copy: Yes.
- Confidence: High for risk-management framing.
- Limitations: Does not certify Vidiolingua's implementation.

### FTC - Voice cloning risk

- Source title: "Preventing the Harms of AI-enabled Voice Cloning"
- URL: https://www.ftc.gov/policy/advocacy-research/tech-at-ftc/2023/11/preventing-harms-ai-enabled-voice-cloning
- What it supports: Voice cloning can be useful but creates impersonation, fraud, creative-rights, biometric-data, and misuse risks; technology alone cannot solve the problem.
- Used in frontend copy: Yes.
- Confidence: High for risk description.
- Limitations: U.S. consumer-protection context; included as high-quality ethical/risk source, not Indian legal authority.

## Claims Intentionally Avoided

- Vidiolingua is legally compliant with all Indian AI laws.
- Vidiolingua is C2PA certified.
- Vidiolingua prevents all misuse or deepfakes.
- Visible watermarking is tamper-proof.
- Metadata cannot be stripped.
- The compliance passport is legal advice.
- Speaker identity is verified unless an actual verification workflow exists.
